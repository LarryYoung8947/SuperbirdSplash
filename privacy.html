Superbird Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 17, 2026

20/20 Studio ("we," "us," or "our") provides Superbird for Android. This policy explains how information is handled when you play Superbird or contact us for support.

Privacy contact: l89471@gmail.com

Information stored on your device

Superbird stores game progress, unlocked levels, checkpoints, scores, and preferences such as sound volume and control settings on your device. This information is used to save your progress and remember your choices. We do not receive these local save files through the game's normal operation or provide a developer-operated cloud-save service.

You do not need to create an account or provide your name, email address, or other contact information to play. The game does not request access to your contacts, camera, microphone, or precise location.

Your device or operating system may back up or restore app data according to your device settings. Those backups are controlled by your device or backup provider, not by 20/20 Studio.

Advertising, analytics, and purchases

The current Android release does not display third-party advertisements or offer in-app purchases. We do not use advertising identifiers for tracking or personalized advertising.

Superbird is built with Unity. Unity Ads, Unity Analytics, and cloud crash reporting are not enabled in the current release configuration. The project is not connected to Unity Cloud for diagnostic-data collection.

Information you send us

If you email us, we receive your email address, any name included with your message, and the information or attachments you choose to send. We use this information to answer your questions, investigate problems, and provide support. Please avoid sending passwords, payment details, or other sensitive information.

Our email provider processes support correspondence to deliver and store messages. We do not sell or rent your personal information or use support messages for targeted advertising. We may disclose information when required by law or when reasonably necessary to protect legal rights or address fraud or security problems.

Google Play and other services

Google Play and Android may process information about downloads, store transactions, device operation, backups, or diagnostics under their own policies and your settings. This processing is separate from Superbird's local save system. Store payment details are handled by the store; the game does not collect payment-card information.

Retention and deletion

Local game information remains on your device until you erase it or clear the app's data. Superbird's Erase Progress option resets campaign progress. To remove local preferences as well, use Android's app-storage controls to clear Superbird's storage. Clearing storage permanently removes local progress and settings. Device backups may need to be managed separately through your backup provider.

We retain support correspondence only as long as reasonably needed to respond to your request, resolve related issues, and meet applicable legal obligations. You can request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information you sent us by emailing l89471@gmail.com. We will respond as required by applicable law. We cannot remotely access or erase saves stored only on your device.

Children's privacy

Superbird does not require players to submit personal information to play and does not include player accounts or player-to-player messaging. We do not knowingly request personal information from children. If you believe a child has sent us personal information through support correspondence, contact us so we can review and delete it as appropriate.

Security

We take reasonable steps to protect information provided to us. No electronic storage or communication method can be guaranteed completely secure. You are responsible for controlling access to your device and its backups.

Visiting this website

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub logs visitors' IP addresses for security purposes. See the GitHub Privacy Statement for details.

The splash page loads fonts from Google Fonts. When your browser requests these fonts, it connects to Google's servers and sends technical information, including your IP address, needed to deliver the fonts. See Google Fonts' privacy information and the Google Privacy Policy. We have not added advertising or analytics scripts to this website. Website hosting and font requests are separate from the Android game's local save system.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the game or our information practices change. The updated policy will be posted on this page with a revised date. Where required, we will provide additional notice or obtain consent.
